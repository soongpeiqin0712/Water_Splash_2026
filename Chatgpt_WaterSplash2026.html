The festival experience

More than a festival.
A whole new vibe.

Water Splash 2026 is a proposed one-day water and music festival for adults aged 18–45. Bring your friends and enjoy water activities, DJ performances, street dance, food and creative experiences in a colourful rainbow atmosphere.

Explore the festival

Two zones. Endless fun.

Start at the Dry Zone to check in, grab a bite and get ready. Then head into the Wet Zone for the main water-and-music party.

💦 Zone 1 — Wet Zone

The main festival area • Water + live entertainment

  • Water Splash Area
  • Water Games Challenge
  • DJ Performances
  • Street Dance Performances
  • Group Dancing & Singing
  • Grand Water Splash Countdown

The main stage faces the central party area so everyone can enjoy the performances and festival atmosphere together.

☀️ Zone 2 — Dry Zone

Welcome, food, creativity & relaxation

  • Registration & ticket checking
  • Food & Beverage Carnival
  • Colorful Face Painting
  • Rainbow Photo Booth
  • Festival Merchandise
  • Changing Rooms
  • Locker Area
  • Rest Area

Dry Zone activities and visitor facilities are planned to operate throughout the festival, from 1 PM to 11 PM.

Fun comes with care

Safety is part of the experience.

The event team will prepare safety arrangements and emergency procedures before the festival. All activities depend on venue approval and safe operating conditions.

🩹

First Aid Station

Trained first-aid personnel to provide immediate assistance.

🛡️

Security Guards

Manage crowds, access points and visitor safety.

🚑

Ambulance Support

Emergency medical transport and coordination.

💧

Water Safety Team

Supervise water activities and safe use of the Wet Zone.

🚪

Emergency Exits

Clearly marked, accessible and unobstructed exits.

⚡

Electrical Safety

Keep cables and electrical equipment away from water.

🌦️

Weather Monitoring

Pause activities during lightning or unsafe weather.

🧯

Fire Safety Team

Prepare for fire risks and coordinate approved displays.

Before you splash

Festival rules & reminders.

Help everyone enjoy the festival safely. Please follow event staff instructions and any additional venue entry conditions.

01Water activities are only allowed in the designated Wet Zone.
02No smoking or vaping.
03No alcohol or illegal substances.
04No sharp objects, weapons or dangerous items.
05Wear comfortable clothes and suitable non-slip footwear.
06No pushing, fighting or inappropriate behaviour.
07Follow instructions from event staff and security guards.
08Do not touch electrical equipment or enter restricted areas.
09Do not throw water directly at people's faces.
10No running or climbing on structures in the Wet Zone.
